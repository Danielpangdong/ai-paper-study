- OpenAI API Docs, Reasoning models: https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/reasoning

Notes:

- The report explains Chain-of-Thought as a reasoning pattern and prompt/reasoning technique, not as evidence of human-like consciousness.
- Product behavior differs: some systems expose reasoning summaries, while others hide or omit raw internal reasoning.
- The two report diagrams were generated with the built-in ChatGPT image generation tool and copied into this report directory.
