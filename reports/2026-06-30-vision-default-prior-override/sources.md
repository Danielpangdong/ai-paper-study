Selection rationale:

- English primary source available on arXiv with open code.
- Not previously covered by this workspace's recent daily-paper reports.
- Strong fit with the automation goal: it explains a long-term issue for multimodal AI reliability in a way ordinary readers can understand.
- The core result is causal, not just behavioral: sparse late attention heads are necessary for prior-knowledge override, while visual grounding remains the default.
